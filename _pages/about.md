  I am a Ph.D. student at the [Data Intelligence (DI) Lab](https://di.postech.ac.kr/di/index.do)
  at Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH), advised by
  [Prof. Hwanjo Yu](https://di.postech.ac.kr/di/mem/professor.do).
research: >-
  I develop **recommendation systems** that capture and elicit users' latent intentions
  from their interaction history. By bringing together multimodal signals, I aim to
  make these hidden preferences clearer, and recommendations more accurate and explainable.
  I also work on **multimodal retrieval**, representing and aligning different modalities
  to connect a user's query with the most relevant document.
